# Sample books

Twenty-one real books for phase 2 (design prototypes) and phase 3 (data format and tests). Together they exercise every tricky case in the master plan. The first eight are from our own shelves; the rest are typical of our library and can be swapped for real copies. Original dates are working dates for testing, to be checked.

- Sets and volumes: 1, 2, 3, 4, 15, 18
- Anthologies: 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 18, 19
- Diglots and triglots: 8, 9, 10
- Disputed or two datings: 2, 6, 10, 11
- BC dates: 9, 14
- No ISBN: 4, 8, 15, 20
- Translation first published: 1, 7, 14, 18
- Author name forms: 9, 11, 12
- Editions grouped under one work, and no longer owned: 12, 13
- Bindings: 4, 7, 14, 16
- Logos, and digital linked to physical: 17, 18, 19
- Wishlist: 21

**Assumptions** (best guesses, fine to change later)

- #5: the Folio Dickens is *David Copperfield* from the 1994 Charles Keeping set.
- #7: Sola Scriptura is where the NKJV came from (a store or ministry); the Bible itself is a Thomas Nelson printing, year assumed.
- #14: an Easton Press *Republic*, year assumed.
- #20: a custom hand-bound *Imitation of Christ* stands in for our custom-made books.
- Bibles list their 66 books as contents, the same way NA28 lists its 27.
