Contents to enter

A laptop page, never loaded by phones. It lists the books whose contents page was photographed while adding, because no catalog listed the works inside.

Open a book: its entry form shows the photo of the contents page above the contents box. Tick "This book holds several works", type or paste the works, one per line ("Author — Title — date", author and date optional), and save. The book then leaves this list.

Open this page in the same browser as the app on this laptop, after the app has synced.
