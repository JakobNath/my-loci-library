Drawn spines

The chosen design: the plain outline, with the dashed edge in a softened version of the cover's color when the book has a cover image, and the plain gray edge when it does not. Here only the photographed books have cover images, so only they get a colored edge. Hold a finger on a spine, or point at it, to see its title and where its color and size came from.
