My Loci Library

The chosen design, as a clickable prototype with the 21 sample books, plus the phone test page. Earlier design rounds are kept in the repository's history.

The chosen design

Forest green with a honey accent on ivory paper, book serif titles, large covers and the ex libris logo. Light and dark mode follow the device; the button in the top bar switches them by hand.

Photo ideas

How covers and spine photos could work, with a second round on taking and showing photos, including a camera prototype to try on a phone.

The logo

An open book with a capital L on the left page and lines of text on the right, after the old "ex libris" bookplates. Saved as app/icons/logo.svg and, as the home-screen icon, app/icons/icon.svg.

Phone test

Checks the camera barcode scan and offline storage on a phone, and gives a short report to copy.
