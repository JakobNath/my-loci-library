My Loci Library: five designs

Five clickable directions for the catalog, all using the same 21 sample books. Each one has the Library (grid and list), the Wishlist, search, a book page, and the batch-add flow with a pretend scan. Open them on a phone and on the laptop. The sun and moon button in each one switches between light, dark and following the device.

Worth trying in each: search for phaedo or politeia; open The Apostolic Fathers (an anthology with a disputed date for the Didache); turn on "No longer owned" and open the Penguin Confessions; and go through "Add books" with each of the three pretend scans.

The five options

Option 1
Card catalog

Cream index cards, typed labels and a library-red rule, with drawer-label navigation on the laptop.
Option 2
Clean modern

The closest to Libib: white space, rounded cards, a teal accent and a coral pop.
Option 3
Warm bookshop

Linen and oak, forest green with terracotta, and the covers standing on wooden shelves.
Option 4
Scholarly

Like a well-set book page: serif type, small capitals, one rubric red, and a list that reads as a shelf list with dates in the margin.
Option 5
Gallery

The covers hung large like prints on a wall, very little else on screen, and a touch of saffron yellow.

Covers are drawn as simple title tiles for now. The real app shows your own cover photos in their place.

Logo and app icon ideas

Each is shown large, at 48 and 24 pixels, and as a home-screen icon at 96 and 48 pixels. Colors can follow whichever design is chosen.

Phone test page
