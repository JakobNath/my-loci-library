My Loci Library: designs

Clickable directions for the catalog, all using the same 21 sample books. Each one has the Library (grid and list), the Wishlist, search, a book page, and the batch-add flow with a pretend scan. Open them on a phone and on the laptop. The sun and moon button in each one switches between light, dark and following the device.

Worth trying in each: search for phaedo or politeia; open The Apostolic Fathers (an anthology with a disputed date for the Didache); turn on "No longer owned" and open the Penguin Confessions; and go through "Add books" with each of the three pretend scans.

Round 3: the playground

Option 7 was the favorite, so round 3 is option 7 with an Adjust button. It changes the main color, the little pop of color, the paper, the title type, shelves, cover size and shadow, corners, spacing, the top bar, the laptop menu and the logo, all live. When you find a look you like, press "Copy link to this look" and send it to each other.

Round 2: quieter and more modern

From the first feedback: options 1 and 3 were the favorites, but they needed a more timeless, modern feel with fewer bright digital colors. These three keep warm neutrals and let the covers carry the color.

Option 6 · from option 1
