Photos: every book has a picture

Round 2: taking and showing photos, with a working camera prototype →

Ideas for how photos work in the app, with mockups. The goal: every book has a picture, and the picture is how we recognize the book, both on screen and on the shelf. The Luther's Works volume uses your real photos; the other books are drawn stand-ins.

So a spine adds about 37 KB per book, or about 4 KB to what each phone keeps offline. For 1,500 physical books that is about 55 MB more in the data repository and 6 MB more on each phone. The 4,000 Logos books have no spines to photograph.

1Taking the photos

In batch add, after the barcode: the cover, then the spine. Each step can be skipped and done later. The spine screen uses a tall, narrow guide, so the phone stays upright and the spine fills the frame. The same four-corner straightening already planned for covers works for spines, and the spine photo is turned upright automatically.

2The Library: four ways to look at it

One switch at the top of the Library, remembered on each device. Tap the buttons inside the phone to try each view.

CoversThe current design: large front covers. Beautiful for books with designed covers; a plain cloth cover like Luther's Works shows only a signature.
Best faceEach book shows whichever picture says more. A plain cover gets its spine instead, standing on a mat in the spine's own color, so the grid stays even. The app can suggest this when a cover photo has very little detail (a simple contrast measure, no AI), and one switch on the book page changes it.
SpinesA wall of spines at their real proportions: thick books look thick. About four times as many books fit on a screen. This is the view to use standing in a used bookstore: what you see on our phone looks like what you see on their shelf.
ShelvesThe same spines grouped by location, in shelf order, so the screen looks like the real bookcase. Handy for finding a book and for checking a shelf against the catalog.

3The book page

Three ways to show both photos on a book's own page.

4Lists and search

In the list view and in search results, a thin spine makes a neat thumbnail that matches what you see on a shelf. Cover thumbnails are kept where a book has no spine photo.

5Sets and series

A multi-volume set shown as a row of spines, numbered, with the gaps visible. At a glance you see which volumes of Luther's Works we have, which are on the wishlist, and which are missing. Tap a missing volume to put it on the wishlist (try it).

6Every book has a picture

Nothing is ever shown without a picture. Until a real photo exists, the app draws one, and it keeps a quiet count of what is still to photograph.

Recommendation

  1. Take two photos of every physical book: cover and spine. Both are optional at entry, but the app keeps asking gently (a "Needs photo" filter and a progress count) until both exist.
  2. Library views: Covers, Best face and Spines, with Best face as the default: it keeps the large-cover look while plain cloth books show their spines. Add Shelves once locations are filled in.
  3. Book page: cover and spine side by side, with the 3D book as a small extra if you both like it (it is a few lines of CSS, no library).
  4. Spine thumbnails in lists and search.
  5. The set view for any book with a set title, such as Luther's Works, Chemnitz's Works and the Ante-Nicene Fathers.
  6. Logos books: the downloaded cover, plus a drawn spine in the cover's own color, so they fit every view.
  7. Photos belong to the copy, not the edition, because two copies can look different (the two Treasury of Daily Prayer bindings). The edition shows its first copy's photos; a wishlist item can carry a photo taken in the store.

What this changes in the plan: the master plan has covers only, so spines, the Best face and Spines views, the set view and the "Needs photo" filter would be added to PLAN.md, and the data format (written next, in phase 3) would include a spine image and a "show spine as this book's picture" setting. Nothing here needs a new library or an outside service. Say which of these you want and I will update the plan before building.
