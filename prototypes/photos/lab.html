2Success rates

Measured against the true edges of each photo. The true edges come from a person, not from the program: in the review below, confirm each automatic result with one tap or correct it. A result counts as right when every corner is within the bar: 2% of the cover's width for covers, 3% of the spine's width for spines (1.5 pixels on a spine 50 pixels wide).

3The new spine flow (try it on your phone)

Spines now have their own mode. The camera shows a tall, narrow frame: fill it with the spine from top to bottom. The app looks for the spine's two long edges, and uses the frame's top and bottom when a hand hides an end. It uses the highest resolution your camera offers. When the edges are clearly found to within 3% of the spine's width, it saves by itself and shows a small result with a one-tap Re-crop; otherwise it opens the editor: two edge lines and two end bars that snap to the nearest edge, a small rotate handle, and the magnifier.
